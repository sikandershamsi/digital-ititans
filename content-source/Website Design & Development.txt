FAQs

What is included in your Website Design and Development Services?
Our services include strategy, UI/UX design, custom development, responsive design, CMS
integration, technical SEO, website testing, launch support, and ongoing maintenance.

Do you build custom websites or use templates?

We specialize in custom website design tailored to your brand, audience, and business
objectives. When appropriate, we also customize trusted platforms like WordPress and Shopify
to meet your specific requirements.

Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every website we build follows a responsive website design approach, ensuring an optimal
experience across desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.

Are your websites optimized for SEO?

Absolutely. Our website development services include technical SEO foundations such as clean
code, optimized site architecture, schema markup, metadata, and Core Web Vitals optimization.

Which platforms do you work with?

We develop websites using WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, and custom technologies
depending on your business goals, functionality requirements, and long-term scalability.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Our website redesign services improve design, usability, speed, SEO, and conversion
performance while preserving valuable content and search visibility wherever possible.

How long does a website project take?

Most business websites are completed within 4–8 weeks, while larger custom or eCommerce
projects may require additional time based on complexity and functionality.

Will I be able to update my website after launch?

Yes. We build websites on user-friendly CMS platforms and provide guidance so your team can
easily manage content, images, blogs, and products without technical expertise.

Do you provide website maintenance after launch?

Yes. Our ongoing maintenance services include updates, backups, security monitoring,
performance optimization, and technical support to keep your website running smoothly.
