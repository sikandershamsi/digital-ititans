Scope of This Privacy Policy
This policy governs the personal information we collect through our website, our email and SMS
communications, our social media profiles, and our client onboarding process. It does not govern the
privacy practices of third-party platforms we link to, and it does not cover data that our clients ask us to
process on their behalf under a separate service agreement.

Information you provide directly
●​ Contact details such as your name, business email address, phone number, and company name

●​ Project details you share in a quote request, consultation booking, or discovery call

●​ Billing and payment information processed through secure third-party gateways

●​ Comments, reviews, and messages you post on our blog or send through our contact forms

●​ Newsletter and marketing preferences, including SMS opt-in consent

Information we collect automatically
●​ IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system

●​ Pages viewed, time on page, referring URLs, and exit pages

Information from third parties
●​ Analytics and advertising platforms such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, and Meta

●​ Publicly available business directories and professional networks

●​ Referral partners who introduce you to our team with your permission

We never request sensitive categories of data such as Social Security numbers, biometric identifiers,

Cookies, Log Files, and Tracking Technologies
Cookies are small text files your browser stores when you visit our site. We use them to remember your
preferences, measure campaign performance, and keep the site secure.

●​ Essential cookies keep core pages, forms, and security features working.

●​ Analytics cookies show us which content earns attention so we can improve it.

●​ Marketing cookies help us measure ad performance and reduce wasted spend.

●​ Server log files record IP addresses and browser data for troubleshooting and fraud prevention.

●​ Web beacons and pixels tell us whether an email was opened or a page was viewed.

You control cookies through your browser settings at any time. Blocking cookies may limit some site
features, though core pages will still load.

We Do Not Sell Your Personal Information
iTitans Digital does not sell your personal information, and we do not rent or trade it for money or other
value. We also do not share your personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising by third parties.

Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent receive extra protection. We never share mobile opt-in data,
text messaging originator information, or consent records with third parties or affiliates for their own
marketing or promotional purposes. That restriction applies to every data category we hold.



How We Share Information
We disclose information only when a clear reason requires it:

●​ Service providers: Hosting companies, email platforms, CRM tools, analytics vendors, and payment
    processors who help us operate. Each one handles your data under contract and only for the task we
    assign.

●​ Legal compliance: Courts, regulators, or law enforcement when a valid legal request applies, or

●​ Business transfers: A merger, acquisition, financing round, or sale of assets may transfer data to a
    successor entity, subject to this policy.

●​ With your consent: Any other disclosure happens only after you approve it.
Data Retention
We keep personal information only as long as the purpose requires. Inquiry records stay on file while we
evaluate and pursue the opportunity. Client project data remains available through the engagement and
for a reasonable period afterward to support warranty, maintenance, and reference needs. Financial
records follow the retention windows that tax and accounting laws set. Once a record loses its purpose,
we delete it or anonymize it.



Data Security
We apply layered safeguards to protect your information, including SSL/TLS encryption across the site,
access controls that limit internal visibility, firewalls, regular software updates, secure hosting
infrastructure, and routine backups. Payment data travels directly to PCI-compliant processors and never
sits on our servers.

No system on the internet achieves perfect security. We work continuously to reduce risk, and we notify
affected individuals promptly when a breach law requires it.



Your Privacy Rights
United States privacy laws give residents of many states meaningful control over personal data. Twenty
states now enforce comprehensive consumer privacy statutes, including California, Texas, Virginia,
Colorado, Connecticut, and Utah. We extend the following rights to every visitor, regardless of where you
live:

●​ Right to know: Ask what personal information we hold and how we use it.

●​ Right to access: Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

●​ Right to correct: Fix information that is inaccurate or incomplete.

●​ Right to delete: Ask us to erase your data, subject to legal exceptions.

●​ Right to opt out: Withdraw consent for marketing emails, SMS messages, targeted advertising, or
    profiling.

●​ Right to non-discrimination: Exercise any right above without losing service quality or pricing.

How to submit a request: Email contact@ititansdigital.com with the subject line "Privacy Request" and
tell us which right you want to exercise. We respond within 45 days and may extend once when a
request requires additional time. We will tell you before we extend.

Verification: We confirm your identity before we release or delete data. Expect us to match details you
provide against records we already hold. Authorized agents may submit requests on your behalf with
written permission.
Opt-Out Signals and Do Not Track
Our website honors Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals and other recognized universal opt-out
preference signals transmitted by your browser or extension. We treat those signals as a valid request to
stop targeted advertising and data sharing. You may also enable Do Not Track in your browser settings,
and you may unsubscribe from any marketing email by clicking the link at the bottom of the message.



Children's Privacy
Our website and services target business decision-makers, not children. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from anyone under 13, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
(COPPA). If you believe a child submitted information to us, contact us immediately and we will delete
the record.



Blog Comments, Forums, and Social Media
Anything you post in a public comment section or on our social profiles becomes visible to others.
WordPress stores your comment, IP address, and browser user agent string to help filter spam, and
Gravatar may display an avatar based on your email hash. Protect yourself by keeping confidential details
out of public posts. We take no responsibility for how others use information you choose to publish.



Third-Party Links and Embedded Content
Our pages link to partner sites, client portfolios, and embedded content such as videos, maps, and social
feeds. Those third parties collect data under their own policies and behave as if you visited their site
directly. Review their privacy notices before you interact with them. iTitans Digital does not control and
does not accept liability for external privacy practices.



International Visitors
We operate from the United States, and we store and process data on servers located here. If you visit
our site from outside the U.S., you consent to transferring your information to the United States, where
privacy laws may differ from the laws in your country.
