Frequently Asked Questions
What does a Website SEO Audit include?
Our website SEO audit reviews crawlability, indexation, Core Web Vitals, metadata, internal linking, structured data, content quality, backlink profile, keyword targeting, competitor performance, and AI search readiness.
What's the difference between a free SEO audit and a professional SEO audit?
A free SEO audit provides a high-level overview of common issues. A professional SEO audit delivers detailed analysis, prioritised recommendations, implementation guidance, and a strategy session tailored to your business.
Do you provide Backlink Audit Services separately?
Yes. Our backlink audit services can be delivered independently or as part of a complete SEO audit, helping you identify toxic backlinks, authority gaps, and link-building opportunities.
What is included in your Competitor SEO Analysis Service?
Our competitor SEO analysis service compares keyword rankings, content depth, backlink profiles, internal linking strategies, topical authority, and organic visibility to uncover opportunities your competitors are leveraging successfully.
Can you identify Google penalties?
Yes. Our SEO penalty recovery audit investigates manual actions, algorithm updates, backlink issues, content quality, and technical errors to determine the likely cause of traffic or ranking declines.
How often should I perform an SEO audit?
Most established websites benefit from a comprehensive audit every 6–12 months, while larger websites or businesses experiencing significant changes should perform audits more frequently.
Do I have to hire iTitans Digital after the audit?
No. Your audit is a standalone deliverable that you own. You're free to implement the recommendations internally, work with another provider, or partner with iTitans Digital for implementation.
How long does an SEO audit take?
Most comprehensive audits are completed within one to three weeks, depending on your website's size, technical complexity, and the depth of analysis required.
