Frequently Asked Questions
How is SEO copywriting different from content writing?
Content writing educates through blogs, guides, and informational resources. SEO copywriting focuses on revenue-generating pages such as homepages, service pages, landing pages, and product descriptions that persuade visitors to become customers.
Do you write with AI?
No. Every page is researched and written by experienced copywriters with SEO expertise. AI can assist with research, but the final copy is written by people because authentic brand voice, expertise, and persuasive messaging cannot be automated effectively.
What pages do you write?
We write homepages, service pages, landing pages, product descriptions, category pages, about pages, and other high-converting website copy. Blog articles and editorial content are covered under our Content Marketing service.
Can you optimize our existing copy instead of rewriting it?
Yes. In many cases, optimizing existing pages delivers faster results than creating new ones. We improve messaging, keyword targeting, page structure, and on-page SEO while preserving the strongest parts of your current content.
Do you write landing pages and conversion copy?
Yes. Our landing page copywriting services are designed to convert both paid and organic traffic using clear messaging, persuasive structure, trust signals, and focused calls-to-action.
How much does SEO copywriting cost?
The cost depends on the number of pages, research requirements, project complexity, and business goals. Most projects are priced per page or as a complete website rather than by word count to ensure quality remains the priority.
Will the copy guarantee higher rankings?
No reputable agency can guarantee rankings. We create copy built around keyword research, search intent, and SEO best practices, but long-term rankings also depend on your website's technical health, authority, and overall SEO strategy.
Do you write for different industries and brand voices?
Yes. Every project begins with research into your business, audience, and industry so the final copy reflects your brand while speaking the language your customers naturally use.
