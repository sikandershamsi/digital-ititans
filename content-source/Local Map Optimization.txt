Frequently Asked Questions
How long does Local SEO take to show results?

Most businesses begin seeing improvements within 2–4 months, depending on competition,
your current Google Business Profile, and your website's local SEO foundation.

Can you optimize an existing Google Business Profile?

Yes. We audit and optimize your existing Google Business Profile, improving categories,
services, descriptions, photos, business information, and local relevance.

How do citations improve Local SEO?

Accurate business citations strengthen Google's trust in your business, improve NAP
consistency, and support better rankings in Google Maps and local search results.

Do Google reviews affect Google Maps rankings?

Yes. High-quality reviews, consistent review activity, and professional responses help improve
customer trust and contribute to stronger local search visibility.

Can you manage multiple business locations?

Absolutely. Our multi-location Local SEO services help businesses manage multiple Google
Business Profiles, citations, and location pages from one strategy.

Will I own my Google Business Profile?

Yes. You always retain ownership of your Google Business Profile and business listings. We
simply optimize and manage them on your behalf.

Do I need a website for Local SEO?

While a Google Business Profile can generate visibility on its own, combining it with an
optimized website significantly improves rankings, authority, and lead generation.

How do you measure local SEO success?

We track Google Maps rankings, local keyword performance, calls, website visits, direction
requests, profile interactions, and overall lead growth.

Can Local SEO help service-area businesses without a storefront?

Yes. We optimize Service Area Businesses (SABs) by configuring service areas, local landing
pages, and Google Business Profile settings to improve local visibility.
