Frequently Asked Questions
What does social media marketing include?
Social media marketing covers strategy, content creation, community management, paid advertising,
influencer partnerships and reporting across platforms like Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok and
YouTube, built around specific business goals.

How much do social media marketing services cost?
Pricing depends on platforms, content volume, paid ad budget and whether influencer partnerships are
included. Most programs run as a monthly retainer scoped around specific goals and audience size.

How long does it take to see results from social media marketing?
Engagement and reach improvements often appear within the first two months. Lead generation and
measurable business results typically build over three to six months as content and targeting are refined.

Which social media platforms should my business use?
Platform choice depends on audience and goals. B2B brands often prioritize LinkedIn; eCommerce and
consumer brands lean toward Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. A strategy call clarifies the right mix.

Do you create content or only manage posting and scheduling?
Content creation is included, not just scheduling. Graphics, video, photography and copy get produced
around brand voice, then published, managed and optimized as part of the same program.

Do you run paid social media advertising too?
Yes. Paid campaigns on Meta, LinkedIn and TikTok are built around clear objectives and audience
targeting, working alongside organic content to close the reach gap organic posting alone cannot cover.
