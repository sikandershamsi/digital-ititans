Frequently Asked Questions

Can you manage an existing Google Ads account instead of creating a new one?

Yes. Our Google Ads management services include auditing, restructuring, and optimizing
existing accounts while preserving your historical campaign data, conversion tracking, and
account ownership.

How do you reduce wasted ad spend in PPC campaigns?

We minimize wasted spend through advanced keyword research, negative keyword
management, audience segmentation, bid optimization, and continuous search term analysis to
improve campaign efficiency and ROI.
Do you manage Performance Max campaigns for eCommerce stores?

Yes. Our Performance Max management services include asset group optimization, audience
signals, product feed improvements, budget allocation, and ongoing performance optimization
to maximize online sales.

Can you optimize our Google Shopping product feed?

Absolutely. We optimize Google Merchant Center feeds by improving product titles,
descriptions, GTINs, categories, pricing, and attributes to increase product visibility and
Shopping campaign performance.

How do you measure PPC campaign success?

We track key performance metrics such as conversions, cost per acquisition (CPA), return on ad
spend (ROAS), qualified leads, revenue, and customer acquisition cost using Google Ads, GA4,
and CRM integrations.

Do you provide landing pages for PPC campaigns?

Yes. We design and optimize PPC landing pages with message matching, fast loading speeds,
clear CTAs, and conversion-focused layouts to improve Quality Score and maximize
conversions.

Can you manage multi-location or nationwide PPC campaigns?

Yes. We create location-specific Google Ads campaigns for local, regional, and nationwide
businesses using geo-targeting, localized keywords, and audience segmentation to reach the
right customers.

Will I receive regular updates on campaign performance?

Yes. You'll receive transparent weekly or monthly reports with campaign insights, optimization
updates, performance metrics, and strategic recommendations to support continuous business
growth.

Can PPC work alongside my SEO strategy?

Absolutely. Combining PPC and SEO services helps increase search visibility, capture more
qualified traffic, and generate both immediate and long-term business growth across search
engines.
